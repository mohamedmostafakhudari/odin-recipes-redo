Egyptian Goulash (Layered Phyllo Meat Pie)

Description

a savory Middle Eastern dish made of crisp, flaky layers of phyllo pastry wrapped around a spiced ground beef filling.

Unlike Hungarian goulash, which is a beef stew, the Egyptian version is a layered savory pie. A signature milk-and-egg wash is poured over the pastry right before baking, ensuring the interior layers remain soft and tender while the top and bottom sheets bake to a perfect, crackly golden brown. It is a staple comfort food served at gatherings, holidays, and Ramadan iftar spreads across Egypt.

Ingredients

  1. For the Meat Filling

    • Lean ground beef: 1 lb (approx. 500g)
    • Yellow onion: 1 large, finely chopped
    • Olive oil or ghee: 1 tablespoon
    • Garlic: 2 cloves, minced (optional)
    • Tomato paste: 1 tablespoon (optional, for richness)
    • Spices: 1 tsp salt, 1/2 tsp black pepper, 1/2 tsp ground allspice (or "Baharat"), and 1/4 tsp ground cinnamon

  2. For the Pastry Layers

    • Phyllo dough: 1 package (16 oz / 500g), thawed completely
    • Ghee or unsalted butter: 1/2 cup, melted (can be mixed with 2 tbsp olive oil)

  3. For the Milk-Egg Wash

    • Whole milk: 1 cup
    • Egg: 1 large egg
    • Salt and black pepper: A pinch of each

Step-by-step guide

  1. Prep the Phyllo Dough

    1. Thaw: Thaw your frozen phyllo dough overnight in the refrigerator.
    2. Rest at room temperature: Pull it out of the fridge 30 minutes before assembling to let it reach room temperature.
    3. Keep covered: Keep the sheets covered with a clean, slightly damp kitchen towel while working so they do not dry out and shatter.

  2. Cook the Meat Filling

    1. Heat the oil: Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil or ghee in a large skillet over medium heat.
    2. Sauté the aromatics: Add the chopped onion (and garlic if using) and sauté for 3 to 4 minutes until soft and translucent.
    3. Brown the beef: Add the ground beef, breaking it apart with a wooden spoon as it browns.
    4. Season: Once the meat is completely cooked through, stir in the salt, black pepper, allspice, cinnamon, and tomato paste.
    5. Finish: Cook for another 2 minutes until fragrant, then remove from the heat and set aside to cool slightly.

  3. Layer the Bottom Crust

    1. Prep the oven: Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
    2. Prep the dish: Brush the bottom and sides of a 9x13-inch baking dish lightly with your melted ghee/butter mixture.
    3. First sheets: Lay 2 sheets of phyllo dough into the bottom of the pan, folding down any excess edges.
    4. Brush: Brush the top sheet generously with the melted ghee.
    5. Repeat: Repeat this process—adding 2 sheets at a time and brushing with ghee—until you have used exactly half of the phyllo package (usually about 10–12 sheets total).

  4. Add the Filling & Top Layers

    1. Add filling: Spread the cooked ground beef mixture evenly over the bottom layers of phyllo dough.
    2. Top layers: Begin layering the remaining half of the phyllo sheets over the beef.
    3. Continue brushing: Continue the pattern of brushing every 2 sheets with melted ghee.
    4. Final brush: Brush the final top layer generously with the remaining ghee to ensure a crisp finish.

  5. Score and Add the Wash

    1. Score the pie: Using a very sharp knife, carefully cut the pie into squares or rectangles all the way through to the bottom of the pan. (Note: It must be cut before baking so the liquid can sink in).
    2. Whisk the wash: In a small bowl, whisk together the 1 cup of milk, 1 egg, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
    3. Pour the wash: Pour the liquid evenly across the entire surface of the pie, tilting the pan so the wash runs down into the cut lines and around the edges.

  6. Bake to Perfection

    1. Bake: Place the pan on the center rack of your preheated oven. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes until the pastry puffs up and the top turns a deep, golden brown color.
    2. Rest: Remove from the oven, let it cool for 10 minutes to set, and re-trace your score marks with a knife before serving.
