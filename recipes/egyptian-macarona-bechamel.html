Egyptian Macarona Béchamel

Description

a baked pasta dish layered with ground meat and a rich creamy sauce.

It consists of layers of penne pasta, a spiced ground meat filling (with onions and sometimes tomato paste), and a thick layer of creamy béchamel sauce on top, baked until golden brown.

Ingredients

  1. The Pasta Base

    • Penne pasta: 1 pound (or elbow macaroni)
    • Salt: 1 tablespoon (for the boiling water)

  2. The Spiced Meat Filling (Insa)

    • Ground beef: 1 pound (90-95% lean works best)
    • Yellow onion: 1 large, finely chopped
    • Garlic: 2 to 4 cloves, minced (optional, for depth)
    • Vegetable oil or olive oil: 2 tablespoons
    • Tomato sauce or passata: 1 cup (or 6 tablespoons of tomato sauce mixed with 1 tablespoon of tomato paste)
    • Warm spices: 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon, 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg, and 1/4 tsp allspice (the signature flavor profile)
    • Salt and black pepper: To taste

  3. The Béchamel Sauce

    • Unsalted butter: 8 tablespoons (1 stick)
    • All-purpose flour: 8 tablespoons
    • Whole milk: 4 to 5 cups (can substitute 1 cup with heavy cream or chicken stock for extra richness)
    • Ground nutmeg: 1/4 teaspoon
    • Black pepper or white pepper: 1/4 teaspoon
    • Salt: 1 teaspoon (or to taste)
    • Eggs (Optional): 1 or 2 eggs, beaten (traditionally whisked into the cooled béchamel to help it set and brown)

  4. The Topping (Optional)

    • Shredded mozzarella or Cheddar cheese: 1 to 1.5 cups (for a bubbly, modern cheesy crust)

Step-by-step guide

  1. Cook the Pasta

    1. Boil the water: Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil.
    2. Cook to al dente: Add the penne pasta and cook it for about 1 to 2 minutes less than the package instructions. It should still have a firm bite, as it will continue cooking in the oven.
    3. Drain: Drain the pasta and set it aside. Do not rinse it with water; the starch helps the sauce stick.

  2. Make the Meat Filling

    1. Sauté the aromatics: Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the chopped onion and sauté for 4–5 minutes until translucent. Stir in the minced garlic for 1 minute.
    2. Brown the beef: Add the ground beef, breaking it apart with a wooden spoon. Cook until it is fully browned and no pink remains.
    3. Season: Stir in the salt, black pepper, cinnamon, nutmeg, and allspice.
    4. Simmer with tomato: Pour in the tomato sauce (or passata). Lower the heat and let it simmer for about 5–10 minutes until the liquid reduces and the flavors blend. Set aside.

  3. Cook the Béchamel Sauce

    1. Make the roux: In a large saucepan over medium heat, melt the butter. Add the flour and whisk constantly for 1–2 minutes until bubbling and slightly golden to remove the raw flour taste.
    2. Add the liquid: Gradually pour in the milk a little at a time while whisking vigorously to prevent lumps.
    3. Thicken: Simmer the sauce over medium-low heat, stirring constantly, for about 5–8 minutes until it thickens to a velvety, coat-the-back-of-a-spoon consistency.
    4. Season & temper: Remove from heat and stir in the salt, pepper, and nutmeg. Let it cool for 5 minutes. (If using eggs, temper them by whisking a small ladle of warm béchamel into the beaten eggs, then stir the egg mixture back into the main pot).

  4. Assemble the Layers

    1. Prep the oven: Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F).
    2. Coat the pasta: Pour roughly one-third of the béchamel sauce directly onto your cooked, drained pasta and mix well. Every noodle should be coated.
    3. Base layer: Spread a thin layer of plain béchamel on the bottom of a 9x13 inch baking dish. Lay down half of the coated pasta in an even layer.
    4. Meat layer: Spread the entire spiced meat filling evenly over the pasta base.
    5. Top pasta layer: Add the remaining half of the pasta over the meat layer.
    6. The blanket: Pour the remaining béchamel sauce evenly over the top, smoothing it out with a spatula so it completely covers the pasta.
    7. Optional cheese: If using mozzarella or cheddar, sprinkle it evenly over the béchamel layer now.

  5. Bake and Rest

    1. Bake: Place the dish in the preheated oven and bake for 35 to 45 minutes until the edges bubble and the top turns a deep, golden brown. (If the top isn't browned enough, you can broil it for the final 2–3 minutes, watching closely).
    2. The Golden Rule (Rest): Remove from the oven and let it rest undisturbed for 20 to 30 minutes before cutting.
